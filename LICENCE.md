## Keeping Twill whole

Twill makes promises. It never loads a page for you. It never clicks, submits or fills
in a form. It never refreshes anything on a timer. It sends nothing anywhere. Those are
not hopes about how people will behave, they are facts about the file published at
https://trashgremlinx.github.io/Twill/ and anybody who wants to can go and check them for
themselves.

An edited copy breaks all of that at once. A few changed lines and a copy could fetch,
click or play the game on its own, and it would still be called Twill, still carry my
name, and still look exactly the same to whoever installed it. The person running it
would have no way of telling. Neither would Wolvden. What comes back on me, on the
Society of Fur, and on everybody using the real thing is a version somebody else wrote.

That is the whole reason for the rules above. They are not about owning it. They are
about the promises staying true and staying checkable.

If you are ever unsure you have the genuine article: install it from
https://trashgremlinx.github.io/Twill/ and nowhere else, check the version on the About screen against the version on
that page, and leave updates switched on. A copy that reached you any other way is not
one I can vouch for.
