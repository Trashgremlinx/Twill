Twill
A Society of Fur toolkit for Wolvden

Hello Society,

I have been building something for us, and it is finally ready to share. Twill is a userscript: it reads the Wolvden page you already have open and tells you more about what is on it.

Hover a gene and a card tells you what it is, where it comes from, what it pairs into and whether it is lethal. Pin two wolves and it works out what their pups could inherit, slot by slot, with shared ancestors and an inbreeding figure for a pairing you have not made yet. Give a wolf lore fields you invent yourself, and when one wolf is bonded to another, both their pages say so. There are nine reference guides kept offline inside it: herbs, scouting, illnesses, prey, battle enemies, befriending, roles, pass rates and pair bonds, all joined up rather than sitting in separate tables.

Twill will never play the game for you.
It never clicks, submits, refreshes or fills in a form. No auto explore, no bump tools, no answers chosen for you in battles or befriending. You still bump your own trades and raffles. It reads the page and changes how it looks, the same way a custom den layout does.
Getting it, in two steps

1. Install Tampermonkey, a free browser extension. Chrome and Edge also need Developer mode switched on, which is a toggle on their extensions page.

2. Click this install link and press Install when Tampermonkey asks.

That is it. After that it updates itself, so you never have to reinstall it by hand. The full write up, with pictures of what it actually puts on screen, a FAQ and the answers to the boring but fair questions, lives here:

Read about Twill

It is free, and you are welcome to pass it to anyone. It is built for the Society but nothing in it is locked to us. It keeps everything in your own browser: no account, no server, nothing about you going anywhere.

If something breaks, or you think of a thing it ought to do, please tell me. Half of what is in it now came from people saying exactly that.
